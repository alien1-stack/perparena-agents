How to read it. Funding APR: the 8-hour rate annualised; positive means longs pay shorts. OI cap: open interest as a share of the market-wide cap. Near 100%, new exposure stops filling, and we have seen books stay crossed there. Spread: best ask minus best bid in basis points of the mid; negative means crossed.
Data: chain RPC api.hideki2.infra.testnet.somnia.network (books, OI, caps) + markets indexer (list, funding). It refreshes every 15 s. Testnet only, not financial advice. Built by day1onearth for the dreamDEX Perps Builder Bounty.
